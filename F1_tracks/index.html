PID Parameters

Adjust the controller terms to change how the car corrects steering based on line displacement.

3.5

Corrects steering in proportion to current error. High values can cause oscillations.

0.05

Corrects accumulated steady-state error. Useful for banked tracks or constant curve offsets.

1.2

Dampens oscillations by predicting future error based on rate of change.

1.5m

How far ahead of the car the virtual sensor looks. Higher values are smoother but cut corners.
